ETL transforms data before loading it into the destination. ELT loads raw data first and uses the destination platform to transform it.

Choosing deliberately

ELT is a natural fit when the warehouse has elastic compute and you want raw data available for reprocessing. ETL can be useful when data must be reduced or protected before it leaves the source environment.

Key idea: The choice is architectural, not moral. Think about compute, governance, latency and replayability together.
